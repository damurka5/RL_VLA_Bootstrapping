Full task, one instruction, no assistance

Each episode starts with an empty gripper and a single instruction. The policy must approach the object, grasp it, lift it, carry it and release it inside the plate or bowl. Each clip shows the overview camera on the left and the wrist camera on the right.

These are strict successes of checkpoint step_28309431 (22.3% strict), evaluated on 2026-09-17 on held-out scenes. The current best checkpoint, step_56072006, scores 41.8%; it has no episode-video audit yet.

The idea

VLA models are usually adapted to a new robot by collecting hundreds of human teleoperation demonstrations and fine-tuning on them. This project asks whether the robot can produce its own training data instead.

1

Acquire skills with RL

The pretrained VLA stays a frozen action prior. A small residual network learns the correction for this robot with GRPO, from task reward only.

2

Keep them with self-generated data

The policy's own successful episodes go into a durable bank of frames and actions. Balanced supervised fine-tuning on that bank brings back skills that later RL erodes.

3

Compose them with staged sparse rewards

The full pick-and-place task is trained from an empty gripper with three ordered binary milestones (approach, pick up, place), each with its own group-relative advantage.

What "without a human dataset" means. No teleoperation or other human demonstrations were collected for this robot. The pretrained SmolVLA did see human robot data during its pretraining. Rewards, success predicates, the simulator and the controller are human-designed. Some intermediate datasets contain actions from scripted controllers: a ground-truth oracle seeded one composition bank, and alignment/yaw controllers bridge the three-stage chains. Removing them from the final policy's lineage is open work.

Method

The robot's action is a world-frame end-effector delta [x, y, z, yaw, gripper] ∈ [-1, 1]⁵. The observation is an overview camera, a wrist camera, the instruction and the robot state. Objects are RoboCasa apple, orange, potato and tomato, with a plate and a bowl as receptacles. MuJoCo Warp runs 512 parallel worlds per GPU.

Main findings

RL alone grounds a pretrained VLA

A frozen prior plus a residual learned reaching and placement on a new embodiment from reward only.

Tolerance decides if vision is enough

The VLA's 3–5 cm localization is too coarse for a ~2 cm grasp and sufficient for 5.7–9.1 cm placement radii.

Self-generated data prevents forgetting

Balanced SFT on the policy's own successes rebuilt a forgotten skill 3.9×. A 4.3× larger slice broke the retention ceiling where more epochs did not.

Staged sparse rewards train the whole task

Once each milestone is reachable, strict full-task success rose from 3.9% to 41.8% on the same evaluator.

Still open

  • Holding the object through the carry. Carry slips and post-lift wrong placements are the main failure, and the grasp rate erodes under continued RL.
  • Retention and composition together. RL on the composed task and SFT on the retention bank still pull against each other.
  • Localization resolution. The frozen encoder's pooled feature reaches the residual without gradient.
  • Scope. One simulated embodiment. A WidowX-200 port keeps the action contract, but batched training on it is not wired yet.
