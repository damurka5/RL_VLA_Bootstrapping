\begin{abstract}
Adapting a pretrained vision-language-action (VLA) model to a new robot commonly requires demonstrations collected on that embodiment. We propose a staged reinforcement-learning pipeline for adaptation without collecting human demonstrations on the target robot. Dense rewards first guide the acquisition of simple skills. Binary refinement and balanced self-imitation consolidate these skills, followed by full-task learning with binary milestones. The milestones provide intermediate credit without requiring a continuous progress function for every phase of the complete task. We evaluate the pipeline in cable-driven parallel robot (CDPR) simulation using a newly initialized residual controller over a pretrained SmolVLA expert, with LoRA adaptation. The controller achieves 70.61\% strict full-task success on development validation. A complementary OpenVLA-OFT experiment on LIBERO-Spatial examines intermediate feedback under a common training setup. Combined dense and phase feedback reaches 65.3\% success versus 61.7\% with terminal-only reward after 160 updates.
\end{abstract}
