The adapted controller completes 1,416 of 2,048 development attempts (69.14\% strict success; Table~\ref{tab:cdpr}). Physical grasp occurs in 92.14\% of attempts and held lift in 89.45\%. Completion rates are 774/1,028 (75.29\%) for plates and 642/1,020 (62.94\%) for bowls. Carry slips (19.43\%) and wrong placements (14.89\%) remain the main failure modes. A matched comparison with the preceding controller yields an 8.35 percentage-point strict-success gain (scene-clustered paired 95\% interval: 6.05--10.69 points; Holm-adjusted $p=0.00005$), with improved grasp and lift. Numerical failures are 24/2,048 (1.17\%); their change is not distinguishable from zero in this comparison.
