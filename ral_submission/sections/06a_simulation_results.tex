\subsection{Execution under one instruction}\label{sec:cdpr_results}
We first tested the supervised fit under the full instruction. It matched its starting policy at five successes in 128 attempts. We then trained with milestone rewards from empty-gripper starts.

We tested the final policy on 512 development scenes outside the training validation panel. We ran four attempts per scene. Each attempt allowed up to 128 decisions. Strict success required a grasp, a held lift, and a deliberate release at the target. We counted slips, wrong placements, and numerical failures as failures. The learned controller acted without assistance during evaluation.

The policy lifted the object in 1,832 of 2,048 attempts. It succeeded in 1,416 attempts (69.14\%). An earlier checkpoint succeeded in 1,245 attempts (60.79\%) on the same scenes. The paired gain was 8.35 percentage points (95\% CI $[6.05,10.69]$).

We also trained a CDPR policy with only the terminal reward. It learned to place objects. The two runs used different starting states, supervision, and success criteria. Measuring the effect of milestone rewards on the CDPR requires a matched comparison.
