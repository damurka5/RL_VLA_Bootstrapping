The separately evaluated controller completes 1,258 of 2,048 attempts (61.43\%; Table~\ref{tab:cdpr}). Held lift occurs in 85.89\% of attempts, but only 71.52\% of held-lift attempts reach strict completion. Transport and release remain the main bottleneck. Completion rates are 68.58\% for plates and 54.22\% for bowls.

A subsequent continuation from 40.06M to 60.03M selected actions logs 723/1,024 strict successes (70.61\%) at its endpoint on the in-run validation panel. Plate and bowl success are 393/512 (76.76\%) and 330/512 (64.45\%). The panel peak is 728/1,024 (71.09\%), selected from 81 readings; the final 20 readings average 69.13\%. These correlated measurements were inspected during training. Separate evaluation of the new endpoint is unavailable, so they establish neither a matched gain over 61.43\% nor a new checkpoint promotion.
