Staged milestone training initially plateaued near 40\% strict success: the retained source scored 36.91\% in repeated development evaluation, and two further 10M-action continuations remained near 37\%. We then retained the bounded residual and continued with the offset-conditioned, pre-clipping likelihood in Section~\ref{sec:rl_loss}. A separate correction-network candidate was not selected. Under repeated evaluation on 512 development scenes with four attempts per scene, the bounded-residual continuation reached 41.16\% after the 2M-action pilot. Later matched comparisons measured 53.47\% at 20M and 61.43\% at 40M.

The latest 60.03M endpoint completes 723 of 1,024 in-run validation attempts (70.61\% strict success; Table~\ref{tab:cdpr}). Physical grasp occurs in 92.48\% of attempts and held lift in 88.28\%. Completion rates are 393/512 (76.76\%) for plates and 330/512 (64.45\%) for bowls. Carry slips (16.70\%) and wrong placements (14.16\%) remain the main failure modes. This progression documents learning beyond the earlier plateau; the latest endpoint uses a different evaluation panel, and the sequence does not isolate the likelihood change from continued training.
