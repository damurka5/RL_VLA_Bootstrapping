\subsection{Additional Embodiment: WidowX-200 Simulation}\label{sec:wx200}
A completed run on a simulated five-DoF WidowX-200 arm yields 33.30\% reaching and 24.02\% pickup success, with 1,024 attempts per skill. Placement from an already-held object succeeds in 72/520 bowl and 293/504 plate attempts (35.64\% pooled). Empty-hand composition succeeds in 32/520 bowl and 41/504 plate attempts (7.13\% pooled). These composed resets align the gripper above the object and omit a distant approach, so the rates are not directly comparable with CDPR full-task success. Earlier skill losses persist through consolidation; composition SFT lowers imitation error without improving rollout success. This initial port exposes unresolved retention and grasp-to-placement transitions.
