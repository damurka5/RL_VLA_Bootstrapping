\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented a staged pipeline for adapting a pretrained VLA to a target robot without collecting human demonstrations on that embodiment. Adaptation begins with dense-reward RL of a new residual controller, followed by binary refinement, successful-trajectory consolidation, and milestone-based full-task learning. Skill evaluations show partial recovery of earlier behavior after balanced self-imitation, and the adapted CDPR controller reaches 70.61\% strict success in simulation development validation. The complementary LIBERO-Spatial experiment compares intermediate and terminal feedback under a common training setup.
