\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
The method begins with skills whose progress the simulator can measure before a complete success occurs. Successful skill trajectories then supply action targets for rehearsal and for learning the complete instruction. Finally, the same skill predicates provide intermediate feedback when the robot acts from an empty gripper. This produced unassisted pick-and-place on the CDPR using automatically generated training data. On WidowX, the policy often failed before it could use the placement skill it had learned from held-object starts. The reported rates are from simulation, and the CDPR development trials repeat one trained policy across scenes. Transfer to hardware remains to be tested.
