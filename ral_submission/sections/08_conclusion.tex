\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented a staged pipeline for adapting a pretrained VLA to a target robot without collecting human demonstrations on that embodiment. Adaptation begins with dense-reward RL of a new residual controller, followed by binary refinement, successful-trajectory consolidation, and milestone-based full-task learning. Skill evaluations show partial recovery of earlier behavior after balanced self-imitation, and the adapted CDPR controller reaches 69.14\% strict success in off-panel simulation development evaluation. The initial WidowX-200 port reveals remaining retention and composition limitations. The complementary LIBERO-Spatial experiment compares intermediate and terminal feedback under a common training setup.
