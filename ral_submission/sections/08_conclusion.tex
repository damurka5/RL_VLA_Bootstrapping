\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented a VLA adaptation pipeline that acquires skills with dense feedback, refines and consolidates them, and learns complete-task execution through milestone credit. SmolVLA experiments show full-task learning and partial recovery of forgotten skills. OpenVLA-OFT experiments support intermediate feedback on LIBERO-Spatial. Together, these results motivate treating exploration, retention, and composition as connected parts of adaptation.
