\subsection{OpenVLA-OFT: Intermediate Feedback on LIBERO}\label{sec:libero_results}
Using the feedback variants in Section~\ref{sec:dense_method}, aggregate runs on the ten LIBERO-Spatial tasks share a 61.0\% initialization and use 300 validation rollouts per endpoint. The primary dense and terminal-only conditions share the terminal reward weight and 160-update budget. A separate low-success task starts at 2.0\%, measured over 1,000 rollouts; both conditions there use the same terminal-outcome group filter.
