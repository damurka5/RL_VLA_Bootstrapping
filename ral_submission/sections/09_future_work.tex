\section{Future Work}\label{sec:future}
Further WidowX-200 work will verify sustained grasp stability with fixed simulator assets and reevaluate saved teachers on both historical and new reset rounds. Stage transitions should require a success floor for every skill, including the newly trained one, and report ordinary-start informative groups separately from assisted starts. Composition should be tested with both prompts on matched states and with actual handoff states before longer joint training. These checks directly address the signal, retention, and transfer conditions in Section~\ref{sec:theory}. Additional experiments will compare stages under matched budgets, repeat training across independent seeds, and evaluate the reserved final-test scenes. Automatic phase detection and progress estimation could reduce manual reward design.
