\subsection{Scope of the Evidence}
The LIBERO comparison concerns reward configurations, whereas the CDPR experiment evaluates the full adaptation procedure. The phase-only LIBERO condition differs in reward weights and training budget, so it does not isolate the effect of removing continuous progress feedback. The CDPR result comes from one training lineage and a development validation panel used during training. Retention measurements cover a small number of rounds. These results establish complete-task execution in the evaluated setting; matched comparisons are needed to quantify the contribution of each training stage.
