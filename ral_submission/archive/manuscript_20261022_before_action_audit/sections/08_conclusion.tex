\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We proposed a training sequence for adapting VLA-based control to new robot embodiments: dense-reward skill acquisition, binary-reward refinement with self-imitation, and staged RL for complete-task execution. The adapted SmolVLA controller achieves 61.43\% strict full-task success in simulation development evaluation on an unconventional cable-driven manipulator. The separate OpenVLA-OFT study on LIBERO examines stage-aware feedback when a trained action policy is already available. \assumed{Physical experiments demonstrate execution of the complete manipulation task on the target robot.} The results motivate evaluating this training sequence across further embodiments, with controlled comparisons identifying the contribution of each phase.
