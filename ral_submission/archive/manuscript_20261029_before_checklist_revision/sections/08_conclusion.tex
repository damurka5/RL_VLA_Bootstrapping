\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented a VLA adaptation pipeline that uses dense rewards only for initial acquisition of a base policy on simple skills. Binary refinement and self-imitation consolidate this policy before staged RL learns the complete multi-stage task. The LIBERO-Spatial reward comparison examines intermediate feedback, and binary milestones provide the composition stage with completion-based credit. Skill evaluations show partial recovery of earlier behavior after balanced self-imitation, and the adapted CDPR controller reaches 70.61\% strict success in simulation development validation.
