\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented a VLA adaptation pipeline that acquires skills with dense feedback, refines and consolidates them, and learns complete-task execution through milestone credit. The LIBERO-Spatial comparisons support intermediate feedback, while milestone-based composition reduces the need for instruction-specific continuous progress functions. Skill evaluations show partial recovery of earlier behavior after balanced self-imitation, and the adapted CDPR controller reaches 70.61\% strict success in simulation development validation. Together, these experiments support treating exploration, retention, and composition as connected parts of adaptation.
