\section{Discussion and Limitations}\label{sec:discussion}
\begin{outline}
\item State precisely which assumptions were tested: conditional sampling, stage separability versus interference, gradient alignment, update size, and exploration-to-deployment mismatch. Theorems explain mechanisms and sufficient conditions; the observed $>50\%$ score remains an empirical result.
\item Interpret staged learning through the measured bottleneck shifts and ablations. Separate observed gains from hypotheses about credit assignment, prior usefulness, and distribution shift.
\item Report scope: one target embodiment, limited objects/destinations, manually specified rewards and calibration, inherited pretrained data, and historical scripted assistance.
\item Address data/compute cost, checkpoint selection, scene reuse, seed coverage, and numerical simulator failures. Development promotion is not final-test evidence.
\item Discuss whether action-prior saturation undermines the expected benefit of a VLA prior; distinguish visual/language feature transfer from action-prior transfer.
\item Physical limitations and transfer claims follow completed trials. Outline the next questions only after identifying what the actual hardware evidence supports.
\end{outline}
