\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented a VLA adaptation pipeline that acquires skills with dense feedback, refines and consolidates them, and learns complete-task execution through milestone credit. With conditional latent-action likelihoods and continued residual training, the CDPR controller reaches 70.61\% strict success in simulation development validation. Skill tests show partial recovery of forgotten behavior, while OpenVLA-OFT experiments support intermediate feedback on LIBERO-Spatial. These results motivate treating exploration, retention, and composition as connected parts of adaptation.
