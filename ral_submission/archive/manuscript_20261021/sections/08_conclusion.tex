\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We studied stage-aware feedback for VLA manipulation through phase-aware dense rewards on LIBERO and protected staged residual learning on a cable-driven robot. The supplied LIBERO comparison reports 65.3\% validation success for primary dense feedback versus 61.7\% for terminal-only training. Matched CDPR development evaluation establishes 61.43\% strict success at 40M selected actions, a 9.86-point improvement over 20M. These results motivate intermediate credit while preserving complete-task evaluation as the empirical criterion. \assumed{Successful physical execution extends the feasibility demonstration to the target robot; the final conclusion will state the verified trial-level outcome and deployment condition.} Controlled ablations and completed hardware records will determine the final scope of the claims.
