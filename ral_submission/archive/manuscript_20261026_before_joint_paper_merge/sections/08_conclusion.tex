\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We proposed a training sequence for adapting VLA-based control to new robot embodiments: dense-reward skill acquisition, binary-reward refinement with self-imitation, and staged RL for complete-task execution. The adapted SmolVLA controller achieves 61.43\% strict full-task success in simulation development evaluation on a cable-driven manipulator. Complementary LIBERO results support structured intermediate feedback with an already trained policy, linking the studies through task progress while leaving their reward density and credit assignment distinct. \assumed{Physical experiments demonstrate execution of the complete manipulation task on the target robot.} Controlled schedule comparisons and further embodiments are needed to establish the contribution and generality of each training stage.
