\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{VLA adaptation.} OpenVLA~\cite{openvla} and SmolVLA~\cite{smolvla} provide pretrained visuomotor policies with different architectures and deployment costs. OpenVLA-OFT studies efficient adaptation through action chunking, parallel decoding, and continuous-action supervised objectives~\cite{openvlaoft}. Our LIBERO implementation starts from an OpenVLA-OFT checkpoint but applies PPO/GRPO updates to discrete action-token likelihoods. Our embodiment-adaptation implementation freezes the VLA during the final RL phase and learns a bounded residual controller. These parameterizations require different probability models.

\textbf{Residual and group-relative learning.} Probe, Learn, Distill (PLD) combines residual reinforcement learning with autonomous data generation and distillation~\cite{pld}. Accordingly, residual learning and success-filtered consolidation are established ingredients, rather than standalone novelty claims here. Our emphasis is the training sequence that links skill acquisition, successful-experience consolidation, and complete-task learning. GRPO originates in language-model reinforcement learning~\cite{deepseekmath}; SimpleVLA-RL brings group-relative online optimization to VLA rollouts~\cite{simplevlarl}. Existing binary-GRPO analyses motivate examining which groups actually provide reward contrast~\cite{mroueh2026,bay2026}.

\textbf{Intermediate rewards and bounded actions.} Potential-based shaping provides conditions for preserving optimal policies~\cite{ng1999}. The phase-aware reward below is an engineering shaping rule and is not asserted to satisfy those conditions. Likewise, protecting achieved stages is a credit modification rather than a general policy-improvement guarantee. Work on clipped-action gradients establishes the importance of distinguishing a latent sample from its executed clipped value~\cite{capg}. We apply that distinction to persistent exploration in the CDPR controller and delimit what it implies for the complete optimization procedure.
