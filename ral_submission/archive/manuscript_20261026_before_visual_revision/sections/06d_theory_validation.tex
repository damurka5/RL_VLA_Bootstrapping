\subsection{Finite-Model Mechanism Checks}
Independent finite-model enumeration verifies the binary-group identities for $G=2,4,8$ at seven probabilities with maximum absolute error $1.2\times10^{-15}$. All 336 enumerated sequential-gate groups at $G=2,3,4$ satisfy the protected-credit sign property; checks over three parameter vectors and three raw-ascent step sizes preserve success. These tests validate the stated finite models, not the neural robot policy.

In a two-decision toy process with persistent offsets, the conditional latent score agrees with the analytic and finite-difference derivatives. At $\theta=0.1$, the derivative is 0.3170, whereas treating exact one-step marginals as independent yields 0.6344. Policy-record likelihood audits, measured mixed-group frequencies, and independent update-alignment measurements are still needed to connect these mechanisms to the reported robot improvement.
