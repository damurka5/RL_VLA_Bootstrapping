\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We proposed a training sequence for adapting VLA-based control to new robot embodiments: dense-reward skill acquisition, binary-reward refinement with self-imitation, and staged RL for complete-task execution. Our phase-aware feedback exposes intermediate progress through dense rewards or milestones. LIBERO experiments show an observed benefit of dense feedback with a trained action policy; the adapted SmolVLA controller achieves 61.43\% strict full-task success in CDPR simulation development evaluation. \assumed{Physical experiments demonstrate execution of the complete manipulation task on the target robot.} Controlled schedule comparisons and further embodiments are needed to establish the contribution and generality of each training stage.
